About RDR Apartments

A Relaxed Stay with the Comforts of Home

At RDR Apartments, we believe every vacation should feel effortless. Our boutique apartments combine modern comfort, privacy, and warm hospitality, giving guests a place to truly relax while exploring the beauty of Curaçao.

Located in the peaceful neighborhood of Santa Catharina, our property offers the perfect balance between tranquility and convenience, with easy access to the island's beaches, restaurants, and top attractions. Mambo and Papagaio beaches just a 15-minute drive away, supermarket 5 minutes away.

See on map →

The property is located 14 km from Queen Emma Bridge and 43 km from Christoffel National Park. Curaçao International Airport is 17 km away. RDR Apartments is situated 12 km from the Curaçao Sea Aquarium. Just a 15-minute drive from Mambo and Papagaio, the island's most beautiful beaches.

Whether you're visiting for a romantic getaway, a family vacation, a diving trip, or an extended stay, we aim to provide a comfortable home base where every guest feels welcome.

Discover Curaçao Like a Local
